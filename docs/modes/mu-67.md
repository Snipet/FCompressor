# Mu 67 (slot 4, `mu-67`)

Mode sheet (01 §8.4 step 6): what Mu 67 runs, every heuristic constant with its source and reason, the measurements
behind them and the revision history. Owned by the Mode's card (M4 in S10). The contracts are 01 §10.7 (descriptor,
traits), 01 §5.2–5.3 (policies, engine, the feedback step, the S10 interface revision X10) and ADR-56 / ADR-63; the
code is `Source/fcdsp/modes/mu-67/`, `Source/fcdsp/engine/stages/gain/ProgressiveKnee.h`,
`Source/fcdsp/engine/stages/ballistics/{TcSelector,MultiStage3}.h` and `Source/fcdsp/engine/stages/colour/TubePushPull.h`.
The unit is D §2.3's Fairchild 660 / 670, a feedback variable-mu (remote-cutoff tube) limiter whose ratio rises with
level, with the six-position TIME CONSTANT switch and the AGC's lateral/vertical (M/S) mode.

## The progressive curve (ProgressiveKnee) [H, fitted]

E §2.2's law, with o = level − T the overshoot:

    r̂(o) = S · o_c · H(o / o_c),   H(u) = u − (1 − e^(−u))   (o > 0; 0 at and below T)

Its local slope S (1 − e^(−o/o_c)) starts at 0 at the threshold and rises monotonically to S. In the loop
(QuadKnee.h's one FB-curve convention) the same law runs at the OUTPUT with the loop gain k = S / (1 − S) in place of
S, so the closed loop's local ratio 1 + k (1 − e^(−(y − T)/o_c)) rises from 1:1 toward 1 + k = 1 / (1 − S). It is
convex and non-decreasing for every S and o_c, so every feedback solve has one root (K2 #5a).

DC THRESH (0 … 10, the knee slot 0 … 40 dB, dial = knee / 4) sets both constants:

| Constant | Law | DC 0 | DC 5 (default) | DC 10 | Reason |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| o_c (onset) | 0.5 + 1.0 · knee dB | 0.5 dB | 20.5 dB | 40.5 dB | "fully anticlockwise gives hard-knee peak limiting, fully clockwise a gentle ratio and knee" (D §2.3 [V S4]) |
| R_max = 1/(1 − S) | 20 − 0.25 · knee | 20:1 | 15:1 | 10:1 | "gradually increases to a ratio of up to 20:1" (D §2.3 [V S3]; JustMastering's 30:1 is [C]) |

01 §10.7 had o_c = 2 + 0.35 · knee and S_max = 0.98 − 0.002 · knee [H], a feed-forward reading: in the loop the law acts
on the output overshoot, whose local ratio rises much faster with a large asymptote (those constants read 2.7:1 one dB
over the threshold and 7.5:1 at 10 dB at the default, 19:1 at 10 dB at DC 0 heading for 50:1), so both were refitted
against the description. The static curve's local ratio (`analysis::localRatio`, `dsp.progressiveknee` `mu67.ratio`):

| DC THRESH | T + 1 dB | T + 2 | T + 5 | T + 10 (RATIO) | T + 20 | T + 40 | asymptote |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | 7.70 | 10.16 | 13.98 | 16.83 | 18.95 | 19.87 | 20:1 |
| 2 | 2.20 | 2.91 | 4.31 | 5.81 | 7.74 | 10.11 | 18:1 |
| 5 (default) | 1.53 | 1.91 | 2.71 | 3.62 | 4.85 | 6.46 | 15:1 |
| 8 | 1.29 | 1.52 | 2.05 | 2.68 | 3.55 | 4.72 | 12:1 |
| 10 | 1.20 | 1.37 | 1.77 | 2.26 | 2.95 | 3.90 | 10:1 |

The default starts "between 1:1 and 2:1 for smaller peaks" and rises with level; DC 0 is a hard-knee limiter (7.7:1
one dB over, 17:1 at 10 dB); DC 10 stays gentle. The rows hold those shapes (rising with level at every DC, falling as
DC turns clockwise, never past the asymptote) and `fb.monotone` over the whole knob (DC 0 … 40 dB every 0.25 dB × four
AC THRESH settings, 644 FB configurations, every 0.1 dB on [−80, +40] dB and every 0.005 dB around T).

**RATIO** (derived, K1 #26 / ADR-56) is the static FB curve's local ratio at T_in + 10 dB for the current DC THRESH
(`muRatioNominal`: a scalar Newton on o_y + k o_c H(o_y/o_c) = 10 dB, within 6e-5 of `analysis::localRatio`); with
`kFlagProgram` the slot prints the live EFF ratio (`analysis::localRatio` at the operating point, 02 §8.1).

**AC THRESH** 0 … 10 over −40 … +24 dBFS (dial = (thr + 40) / 6.4, 10 = no compression, not inverted: the S4 lead
ruling). Default **−18 dBFS (dial 3.4)** [H, fitted; 01 §10.7 had 0 dBFS]: the operating level at unity INPUT, 0 VU =
+4 dBu = −18 dBFS (ADR-59), which is also the host default threshold (Clean's, FET 76's), so a fresh Mu 67 compresses
programme at 0 VU as the others do. It also keeps the colour's meter-truth budget (below) at the probes' levels.

**The feedback solve.** `ProgressiveKnee::solveFb` is FeedbackZdf.h's safeguarded Newton (the midpoint where an
iterate leaves the bracket) honouring `FbAffine::base` (X10), with three changes for cost: the bracket's upper end is
A + B k max(0, o′ − A) (H(u) ≤ u, no curve evaluation), the iteration starts on the root of the map on the curve's
linear asymptote (H(u) ≥ u − 1: at or below the root), and a lane stops once its step is within 2^−21 of the
increment's scale and stays frozen (so a lane never depends on the others: `analysis::staticGr` and the engine's
`tgtDb` agree bit for bit, `dsp.analysis`). 01 §10.7 names `FeedbackZdf<ProgressiveKnee>`; it remains a valid wrapper
of the same law and `dsp.progressiveknee` holds both to 200-step bisection (≤ 1e-5 dB, every branch), but its fixed
six steps cost three to five times as much per solve (the probe's timing NOTE: about 20–25 ns for a ballistics solve,
40 ns for the static one, against 100 ns), and Mu 67 runs two to five solves per sample (TcSelector's branches and the
S10 carry). The law's precision: H below u = 1/2 is its Taylor series (the direct form cancels there), relative error
≤ 7.1e-7 over u ∈ [1e-6, 80], seam 3.5e-7.

## TIME: the time constants (TcSelector)

| TIME | Attack (published) | Release | Ballistics |
|---|---|---|---|
| TC1 | 0.2 ms | 0.3 s | SmoothBranching |
| TC2 (default) | 0.2 ms | 0.8 s | SmoothBranching |
| TC3 | 0.4 ms | 2 s | SmoothBranching |
| TC4 | 0.8 ms (D §8.2: 0.8 per S3 [C: 0.4 per S4]) | 5 s | SmoothBranching |
| TC5 | 0.4 ms | programme: 2 s peaks, 10 s multiple peaks | MultiStage3 (two stages) |
| TC6 | 0.2 ms | programme: 0.3 s peaks, 10 s multiple peaks, 25 s consistently high programme | MultiStage3 |

TIME is a `rel` list (01 §10.2); TC5 / TC6 carry `kTagTc5` / `kTagTc6 | kTagProgram`, which `TcSelect` reads. The switch
is not part of the kernel key: TC4 ↔ TC5 swaps the ballistics inside one engine through AutoSwitch's 20 ms hand-over
(the other path seeded from the running GR; MultiStage3 seeds charged), so a switch while compressing never steps the
gain (`dsp.zipper` detent edges; `dsp.tcselector` `mu67.switch`: 5e-5 dB per-sample steps at TC4 → TC6 → TC4).

**Attack (ADR-63).** The published attack is the closed loop's. The progressive loop's gain varies with level (about k
at the step's start, the static point's local gain at its end), so no single 1 + k converts it: `physical()` sets the
open-loop τ to the published time × `Mu67::attackOpenLoopFactor`, the factor that gives the continuous-time loop the
published attack on the D2 step (T − 20 → T + 20 dB, the 63 % point of the GR's dB change):
τ_open = τ_pub / I, I = ∫₀^{r63} dr / (k o_c H((20 − r)/o_c) − r) (Simpson, 32 panels; a linear loop gives FET 76's
1 + k). Open-loop τ at 0.2 ms: 3.99 ms (DC 0), 1.33 ms (DC 5), 0.69 ms (DC 10). Measured through the engine
(`dsp.progressiveknee` `mu67.attack`, D2's t63 on the tapped GR):

| Published | 192 kHz, DC 0 / 5 / 10 | 48 kHz, DC 0 / 5 / 10 |
|---|---|---|
| 0.2 ms | +1.2 / +1.4 / +1.1 % | +4.9 / +5.5 / +4.3 % |
| 0.4 ms | +0.6 / +0.7 / +0.6 % | +2.5 / +2.8 / +2.2 % |
| 0.8 ms | +0.3 / +0.4 / +0.3 % | +1.2 / +1.4 / +1.1 % |

(the discrete loop's excess is O(1/N): 0.2 ms is 9.6 samples at 48 kHz; the spec row holds every TC × DC within 5 % at
192 kHz; `dsp.srsweep` holds the default attack to 48 kHz's across 44.1–192 kHz.) The releases are not converted:
measured the hardware way (the level drops below the threshold) they run with the loop open, at the one-pole's τ
(`dsp.time`: 0.300 / 0.800 / 2.00 / 5.00 s for TC1–TC4).

**The programme network (MultiStage3) [H, fitted].** The Fairchild's TC5 / TC6 network: a main capacitor charged by
the rectifier and further capacitors charging from it through resistors, so they fill only when the programme keeps the
first charged, and then hold the control voltage up. Modelled as a serial chain of one-poles in the GR domain (stage 1
smooth-branching toward the target; stage 2 toward stage 1, charging τ_C2 while below it, else releasing τ_R2;
stage 3 likewise toward stage 2), the applied GR the max; in the loop every stage is an affine map of r̂, so the GR is
the max of their roots (01 §5.3), with the S10 sub-ulp carry on the winning stage (lead revision 3). D §2.3's times are
the recoveries the network gives (t63 of the GR's dB fall, the TIME spec's law), not its stage times: a serial network
releases slower than its last stage alone (the stage behind it is still falling), so the stage times were fitted to
the recoveries after a peak (a burst up to 0.2 s at T + 20 dB), multiple peaks (2 s of programme) and consistently
high programme (20 s and more):

| TIME | τ_R1 | τ_C2 | τ_R2 | τ_C3 | τ_R3 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| TC5 | 2.0 s | 3.0 s | 7.7 s | – (stage 3 follows stage 2) | – |
| TC6 | 0.3 s | 1.0 s | 8.0 s | 8.0 s | 16.0 s |

Measured through the engine (`dsp.tcselector` `mu67.program`, release t63 after a burst at T + 20 dB):

| Burst | 20 ms | 200 ms | 1 s | 2 s | 5 s | 20 s | published |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| TC5 | 2.00 s | 2.00 s | 4.67 s | 6.53 s | 8.82 s | 9.96 s | 2 s peaks, 10 s multiple peaks |
| TC6 | 0.30 s | 0.30 s | 5.03 s | 10.5 s | 18.8 s | 24.7 s | 0.3 s peaks, 10 s multiple, 25 s programme |

(spec rows: the three published recoveries within 15 %, non-decreasing with the burst, TC6's tail to 1/e² ≥ 25 s: 42 s.)
The charges keep a single peak's recovery at stage 1's time: a 20 ms peak on TC6 leaves stage 2 near 20 % of the GR,
decaying at τ_R2. `dsp.time`'s program-dependent rows (0.5 s at T + 20 dB): TC5 2.41 s, TC6 2.22 s, within the other
positions' span [0.3, 5] s. The FB carry: the network from a charged state with the loop open tracks the exact
three-stage recurrence within 1e-6 dB over 60 s at 48 kHz and 30 s at 384 kHz (the plain float recurrence: 0.065 /
1.2 dB); a float tie between two stages' roots goes to the later stage (its value is then the larger one; giving it to
the earlier stage capped the slow stage at that value, 7.6e-5 dB lost on TC6, M4).

## LAT/VERT and LINK

`stmode` L/R | LAT/VERT is the universal stereo code (0 STEREO, 1 M/S): the host Router encodes lanes 0–1 as lateral (M
= (L + R)/2) and vertical (S = (L − R)/2) and decodes after the colour; a change is a kernel-key crossfade (01 §5.5).
LINK ties the two lanes' GR (LinkMax after the per-lane solve), so LAT/VERT + LINK is the Herchild's linked M/S.
`dsp.progressiveknee` `mu67.latvert` (EngineHost, ECO and STD): a mid-only input comes out with L = R bit for bit and a
side-only one with L = −R, each compressed 5.7 dB at T + 10; with the mid at T + 10 and a quiet side, LINK gives the side
the mid's GR (within 0.001 dB) and IND none.

## TubePushPull: the colour [H]

A memoryless odd residual on the wet signal (after the gain element), of u = k x, k = kIn · 10^(INPUT/20):

    y = x + O(GR) · (tanh u − u) / k,     O(GR) = kOddStatic + kOddGr · g / (g + kGrHalfDb),   g = max(0, GR)

| Constant | Value | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| kIn | 0.15 | the meter-truth budget (below) |
| kOddStatic | 0.01 | the transformers, with no GR |
| kOddGr | 1.0 | the pair's term at full bias |
| kGrHalfDb | 12 dB | the GR at which the pair's term is half way |

Balanced push-pull cancels the even orders and the pair's distortion grows with GR (D §2.3 [U]); the model keeps the
even orders at zero (a small even term, 50 dB under H3, put a ripple on dsp.time's quadrature envelope that moved the
audio-measured release by 3.7 samples between Qualities, over the 1.5-sample row). At 0 dBFS into the colour: H3 −94.6
dB with no GR, −65.0 / −61.3 / −58.6 dB at 5 / 10 / 20 dB of GR, H5 about 50 dB lower, no H2; INPUT −20 dB lowers H3 by
40 dB. The level is set by the probes' meter truth (character: `tap_vs_audio` ≤ 0.1 dB per sample): the binding case is
dsp.time's 40 dB drop, where ADAA-1's segment mean carries the loud sample's residual into the first quiet one
(O u_loud³ / (12 u_quiet): 0.11 dB at kIn = 0.2, 0.063 dB at 0.15); the steady compression of the fundamental stays
under 0.02 dB on dsp.static's staircase. ADAA-1 on the residual (Adaa.h's scheme), k gliding across each call (no step
at an INPUT detent), digital silence exactly silent, colourStatic = false (the COLOUR view draws the operating point's
GR).

## Internals (UiFrame, 01 §10.7)

From the louder of lanes 0–1, at the ballistics' applied GR:

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| BIAS (V, the history internal) | the grid bias for the applied GR, GR / 2 dB per V [H], 0 … 20 V |
| EFF RATIO | the live local ratio of the static FB curve at the operating point that produces the applied GR, 1 + k (1 − e^(−o_y/o_c)) with k o_c H(o_y/o_c) = GR (at T + 10 dB it equals RATIO, within 5e-8) |
| TC WEIGHT | the share of the GR the programme network holds above the peak stage, (GR − r1) / GR, × TcSelector's blend weight: 0 on TC1–TC4 and while stage 1 holds a peak, → 1 once a sustained programme has charged the slow stages (0.999 after 20 s + 2 s on TC6) |

## Cost

`ctBudgetNsPerSample` 90 (S13 H1b, from measurement with the editor open; was E §3.7's 60). The control path runs two
to five solves per sample; the bench (`fcmp_bench --mode mu-67`, measured by M4 on a loaded machine, so indicative
only) read about 2.4× the old budget at STD 48 kHz detached against FET 76's 1.1× (see the S10 handoff for the numbers
and their conditions). Before the solver changes (FeedbackZdf's fixed six steps) it read about 10×. S13 H1b measured
57.8 / 81.9 ns/sample/ch at STD 48 kHz detached / attached and 79.4 / 103.8 at HQ (against 120, the HQ rule). The editor
adds +24 ns: the static target (ProgressiveKnee's Newton at every sample, for `tgtDb`) and EFF RATIO's 32-step bisection
at every internals latch; neither has an exact cut behind the frozen interfaces ([budgets.md](budgets.md)).

## Known limits

- The progressive law is E §2.2's, run at the output (the FB convention); the real unit's side chain is a linear-
  amplitude rectifier into a dB-linear element, whose loop gain keeps rising with level instead of approaching k.
- DC THRESH changes the curve's shape only; on the hardware it is a bias control and also moves the onset.
- The programme network is a serial max-of-stages chain, not the coupled RC network (whose node voltage is a weighted
  mix): the recoveries are fitted, the shape between them is the chain's.
- The transformers' LF weight (D §2.3 [U]) and any even-order imbalance are not modelled; the colour is memoryless.
- `EngineTelemetry::attackNowMs` reports the open-loop τ (ADR-63), as FET 76's does.
