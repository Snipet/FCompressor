## Lead revisions at the S2 base

1. **Standing rules from S0/S1** (see `docs/sprints/s1.md` "Lead revisions" 1–5 and its Outcome): the full
   `FCMP_WARNING_FLAGS` list with `-Werror` (no `default:` over `Pid`); `FCDSP_NONBLOCKING` (from `core/Rt.h`) repeated
   on every out-of-line definition of an annotated declaration; probe exit-code rules; ≤ 2 build directories, `-j6`,
   delete them after recording results (~28 GB free).
2. **F3 — engine contract details fixed at FZ0 (R-F0 #2, #3, #6).** Gain computers take the per-sample
   `LevelCtl{thrDb, slope, kneeDb, s2ThrDb}` (`G::target(c, x, l)`, `G::solveFb(c, x, l, a)`, `S2::combine(c, s, r, x, l)`),
   built by `ModeEngine` from its smoothers each sample and by `staticGr` from `EngineParams` unsmoothed; `design()`
   never bakes those four values into `Coeffs`. `S2::seed` exists; `ScShapePolicy` and `LinkPolicy` are in the
   `static_assert`; `ModeEngine` declares `friend M;` so a Traits `internals()` hook can read `det_`/`bal_`/`s2_`.
3. **F3 — `dsp.registry` must ALWAYS call `note("provisional", jsonArray(keys))`, even when the list is empty** (FunkGui
   `golden.py adopt` refuses every Mode-scoped candidate otherwise).
4. **F3 — long time constants.** `alphaFromTau` loses accuracy for very long τ at high rates (F1: 0.3 % at 1e5 samples,
   10 % at 3.8e6). Ballistics should carry `1 − α` (or an equivalent well-conditioned form) for releases, so Opto/Mu
   Modes' multi-second releases at 192–384 kHz stay within spec. `ScopedFtz` only orders values loaded and stored inside
   its scope (F1 finding); the Rig must keep processing inside the scope.
5. **F3 — Clean.** `CleanDesc.cpp` (F2) defines `extern constexpr ModeDescriptor kClean` in `fcdsp::modes` with
   `provisional = true`; your `Clean.h`/`Clean.cpp` declare `extern const ModeDescriptor kClean;` and end with
   `FCDSP_DEFINE_MODE(Clean);` at global scope. Uncomment slot 0 only in `Modes.def`.
6. **F6 — `proc.osref`** lives under `Tools/probes/plugin/` and links JUCE (the `agent` preset, not `dsp`). Repeat
   `FCDSP_NONBLOCKING` on the `Oversampler` definitions. The four latency constants you choose become v1-forever at FZ2.
7. **G3.** Worktree `/Users/seanfunk/audio/libraries/FunkGui.wt/s2-g3` from v0.2.0. `Canvas::textWidth` must call
   `text::width` so the two stay bit-identical (G2 note). The atlas lacks U+2026 and U+2212 until G4; do not add glyphs.
   FZ1 froze FunkGui's public declarations; private sections marked provisional may grow. [XV] uses the read-only
   FCompressor worktree `/Users/seanfunk/audio/plugins/FCompressor/.claude/worktrees/ro-s2-g3` (detached at this base).
