FCOMPRESSOR

THE PLUGIN'S OWN DSP AND EDITOR, RUNNING IN THIS BROWSER

A DRUM LOOP PLAYS THROUGH THE COMPRESSOR. SOUND STARTS WHEN YOU CLICK.

SOURCE BUILT-IN LOOP OR DROP ONE ON THE PAGE. IT STAYS IN YOUR BROWSER.

WHAT DIFFERS FROM THE PLUGIN

  • BYPASS, IN THE DISPLAY ROW, COMPARES WITH THE DRY LOOP AT THE SAME LATENCY, AS IN THE PLUGIN.
  • NO PRESET IMPORT OR EXPORT. YOUR OWN PRESETS LAST UNTIL THE PAGE IS CLOSED.
  • NO SIDE-CHAIN KEY INPUT.
  • THE DSP LOAD IN SETTINGS SHOWS A DASH.
  • WITH NATURAL SCROLLING ON A MAC, THE WHEEL TURNS A VALUE THE OTHER WAY. A NOTCHED WHEEL MOVES A STEPPED CONTROL ABOUT TWO STEPS.
  • TYPED VALUES TAKE PLAIN KEYS ONLY (NO INPUT METHOD, NO DEAD KEYS).
  • A QUALITY OR LOOKAHEAD CHANGE MAY CLICK ONCE: THE ENGINE IS REBUILT ON THE AUDIO THREAD.
  • DESKTOP BROWSERS WITH WEBGL2. NO SCREEN READER SUPPORT.
